## 2025-05-14 - [Branding and Safety as UX]
**Learning:** In documentation-centric repositories where the README acts as the primary user interface, professional branding (correct spelling) and high-visibility safety warnings are critical for establishing user trust and ensuring accessibility of life-saving information.
**Action:** Prioritize correcting primary heading typos and placing critical safety warnings using `[!WARNING]` callouts at the very top of the page.
