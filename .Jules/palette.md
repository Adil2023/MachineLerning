## 2024-05-23 - [Documentation as UI]
**Learning:** In documentation-centric repositories, the README.md is the primary user interface. UX improvements should focus on information hierarchy, discoverability (Table of Contents), and critical safety information (placed prominently at the top). Correcting branding typos is essential for establishing credibility and trust, especially for safety warnings.
**Action:** Prioritize README.md structure and safety warnings in hardware/documentation projects.
