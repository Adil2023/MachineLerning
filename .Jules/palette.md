## 2026-05-23 - Holistic Documentation UX: Branding and Safety
**Learning:** In hardware-focused repositories, UX is delivered through the README. This includes both baseline professionalism (correct branding/spelling in headings) and critical functional safety (prominent battery warnings). A typo in the main heading undermines the credibility of safety-critical instructions.
**Action:** Always verify primary branding/headings when implementing documentation-based UX improvements. High-visibility safety warnings should be paired with professional presentation to maximize effectiveness.
