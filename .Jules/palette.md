## 2025-05-14 - Documentation-Centric UX Improvements
**Learning:** In repositories where the primary interface is documentation (e.g., hardware design projects), the README.md serves as the user's first point of interaction. Professional branding (correct spelling in headings) and prominent safety warnings are critical for establishing trust and ensuring user safety before technical details are consumed.
**Action:** Prioritize correcting primary headings and placing critical safety warnings at the very top of documentation files in hardware-critical contexts.
