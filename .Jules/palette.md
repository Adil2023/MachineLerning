## 2025-05-14 - Documentation as UI in Hardware Projects
**Learning:** In documentation-centric or hardware repositories, the README.md acts as the primary user interface. UX improvements here, such as correcting branding typos and adding high-visibility safety warnings, are critical for establishing trust and ensuring user safety.
**Action:** Prioritize information hierarchy and prominent safety callouts (using GitHub's `[!WARNING]` syntax) in repositories where the main deliverable is a design or guide.
