## 2025-05-14 - Documentation as the Primary Interface
**Learning:** In documentation-centric hardware projects, the README.md serves as the primary user interface. UX improvements here should focus on information hierarchy, branding credibility, and critical safety warnings placed prominently to ensure user trust and physical safety.
**Action:** Prioritize correcting branding typos and placing safety warnings at the very top of documentation for hardware projects to establish trust before technical details are presented.
